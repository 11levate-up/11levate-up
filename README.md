# Shivam Sharma

### NLP & Voice AI Researcher — Full-Duplex, Code-Switched Spoken Systems for Low-Resource Languages

I research AI systems for **multilingual speech and language understanding**, focused on a
gap that current large-scale speech and language models still struggle with: natural,
real-time spoken interaction in code-switched, low-resource languages such as Hindi-English.
My work sits at the intersection of speech foundation models, code-switched NLP, and agentic
voice systems.

---

## Research Focus

- Full-Duplex Spoken Dialogue Systems
- Code-Switched Speech & Language Processing (Hindi-English)
- Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR) for Low-Resource Indic Languages
- Speech Representation Learning & Foundation Models
- Agentic AI for Voice-Driven Systems

---

## Current Work

**Full-Duplex, Code-Switched Spoken Agentic Systems for Low-Resource Indian Languages**
Investigating how full-duplex speech-language models — capable of natural, real-time
listening and speaking — can be adapted to code-switched, low-resource Indic speech, where
existing systems still show significant performance degradation.

**Code-Switched ASR & Language Understanding**
Building and benchmarking ASR and language models on Hindi-English code-switched speech and
text, with a focus on closing the reasoning/quality gap that current multilingual LLMs show
when moving from high-resource to code-mixed input.

**Agentic Voice AI**
Combining speech understanding with action-taking agents to build spoken systems that go
beyond transcription — understanding intent and executing tasks in code-switched language.
